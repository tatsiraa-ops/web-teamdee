# TEAMDEE

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People planning team apparel for organizations, schools, sports teams and events. They need to select materials and production techniques, find design ideas, and prepare an order.

## Product Purpose

Help customers understand custom apparel options and contact TEAMDEE with useful project details. The user confirmed on 2026-10-27 that the article experience should support both buying decisions and inspiration, with SEO as a priority.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing Vite/Handlebars project exports a WordPress theme. Twelve established articles are stored in WordPress; the local legacy-posts catalog contains their metadata, not their full content. Preserve existing URLs, factual article content and CMS ownership. Draft content must not become published content through a redesign. No deployment without user authorization.

## Brand Commitments

TEAMDEE identity and existing brand assets remain. The user requests distinctive professional apparel expertise, not generic AI-looking UI. This redesign concerns article surfaces; the homepage and floating contact widgets remain unchanged.

## Evidence on Hand

src/data/legacy-posts.json contains published article metadata. src/data/articles.json and the article template also support local drafts. Customer and garment imagery exists under public/img. Do not invent authors, testimonials, dates or commercial claims.

## Product Principles

- Help readers make an apparel decision and find relevant inspiration.
- Preserve truthful content and existing searchable URLs.
- Treat mobile reading and keyboard navigation as core functionality.
- Keep promotional actions subordinate to the reading task.
